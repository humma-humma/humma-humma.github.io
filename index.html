Research

My research interests lie in 3D/4D vision, representation learning, generative modelling, and reliable inference for structured and multimodal data. I am especially interested in human-object and human-scene interaction, generative priors for reconstruction, uncertainty under occlusion, and learning in low- and missing-data regimes.

Publications

VISTA: Video-Injected Stylized Text-to-Animation [Master thesis]

Monseej Purkayastha, Anindita Ghosh, Philipp Slusallek
Multimodal Human Motion Analysis Workshop at CVPR (MOMA), CVPR 2026
A multimodal diffusion model that combines CLIP text conditioning with ViViT video conditioning for stylized 3D human motion generation. VISTA outperforms the prior video-conditioned method SMooDi (77.8% vs. 68.1% style recognition accuracy, 2x lower FID) and reaches 70% zero-shot style accuracy in text-video style transfer.

An Ensemble Approach to Hostility Detection in Hindi Tweets

Santosh Rajak, Monseej Purkayastha, Amitabh Deb, Ujwala Baruah
ICCCSP 2023, Springer
A two-stage pipeline for detecting hostile posts in Hindi (Devanagari) tweets. A Hinglish BERT-based binary classifier first flags hostile content, then soft-voting ensembles (SVM, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Gradient Boosted Trees, XGBoost) on pretrained Hindi FastText embeddings assign fake, hate, defamation and offensive labels, reaching F1-scores of 80% (fake), 79% (offensive), 76% (defamation) and 69% (hate) on the test set.

Projects

Persistent 3D Scene Memory for Vision-Language-Action Policies

A confidence-aware, world-aligned 3D memory and geometry-token adapter for SmolVLA, integrating three-camera RGB, proprioception, and native 12D control in RoboCasa, fine-tuned on 42K+ demonstration actions. Reduced held-out imitation RMSE by 33.7% versus zero action and improved action error by 14.9% over ungated persistent geometry across 480 controlled occlusion and scene-change trials.

3D Reconstruction: Incremental SfM & RGB-D SLAM

A multi-view SfM pipeline using SIFT/AKAZE matching, PnP registration, and bundle adjustment with sub-pixel reprojection accuracy across up to 51 cameras, extended to RGB-D SLAM with learned-frontend loop closure using SuperPoint+LightGlue.
